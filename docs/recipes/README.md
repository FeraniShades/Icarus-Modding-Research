# Practical Recipes

These guides turn verified research into procedures that another modder can follow. They explain both the intended result and the clues produced by common mistakes.

- [Turn a decorative deployable into storage](decorative-item-to-storage.md)
- [Change materials, textures, and mesh references](materials-textures-and-meshes.md)

## Illustrated PDF Walkthroughs

- [Landing Pad material variant walkthrough](walkthroughs/landing-pad-material-variant-walkthrough.pdf) - creates a dark landing-pad variant, follows the material and mesh references, and finishes with the Blueprint child conversion required to preserve landing-pad behavior.
- [Texture replacement walkthrough](walkthroughs/texture-replacement-walkthrough.pdf) - follows a Homestead Silo from mesh to material instances and texture maps, edits the textures, cooks replacements in Unreal Engine 4.27.2, and reconnects renamed assets.

Recipes describe known-good patterns, not universal one-click conversions. Icarus updates may change assets or table rows, and sibling variants should always be compared before applying a bulk edit.
