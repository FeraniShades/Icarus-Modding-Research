# Homestead Expanded

Homestead Expanded gives practical functions to decorative Homestead furniture while keeping the objects visually at home in the DLC.

## Features Demonstrated

- Filtered themed storage for honey, jars, cheese, and hanging meat.
- Preservation behavior for selected food storage.
- Butter churn crafting with inventory output.
- Fabric processing at the spinning wheel.
- Processing-state animation on the spinning wheel.
- Higher-tier sewing and kitchen progression.
- Functional weather vane, well, pitchfork, and expanded animal-feed silo behavior.
- Careful DLC requirement handling.
